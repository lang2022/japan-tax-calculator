About RefundJapan

A free, multilingual Japan tax refund calculator for international travelers.

What this tool does

RefundJapan helps visitors to Japan estimate how much consumption tax they can get back through tax-free shopping. It supports six languages and shows results in your home currency using live exchange rates.

How the estimate works

  • Standard tax rates: 10% for general goods, 8% for consumables.
  • Service fees: An optional ~1.55% deduction, commonly charged by major department stores.
  • Live exchange rates: Fetched from a public currency API so your refund is shown in a familiar currency.
  • Eligibility checks: The ¥5,000 minimum spend threshold and the ¥500,000 cap are applied automatically.

What this tool is not

RefundJapan is an independent project and is not affiliated with the National Tax Agency, the Japan Tourism Agency, or any store. Results are practical estimates for reference only - the final amount always depends on store policy and the rules in effect on the day of purchase.
