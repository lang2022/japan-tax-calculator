Japan Tax-Free and Tax Refund Guide 2026

If you are trying to work out how much tax refund you can get back in Japan, this calculator gives you a practical estimate after tax-free rules, item category, and department store fees are taken into account.

1. Minimum spend

In most standard tax-free shopping scenarios, you need to spend at least ¥5,000 before tax in the same store on the same day. This is the minimum spend threshold used by the calculator.

2. Refund rate

The refund rate is not always the same in practice. General goods are commonly estimated at 10%, while consumables are often estimated at 8%. Your actual result can still vary depending on how the store handles categories and packaging.

3. Department store fees

Some major department stores deduct a tax-free handling fee - often around 1.55% - before you receive your final refund amount. That is one of the main reasons why the amount you get back can be lower than the headline tax rate.

Estimated refund = (Pre-tax amount × tax rate) - (Pre-tax amount × fee rate)
