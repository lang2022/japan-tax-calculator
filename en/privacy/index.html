Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). The purchase amounts you enter into the calculator are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to or stored on our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Exchange rate API: We fetch real-time currency conversion data from a public exchange rate provider. No personal data is shared with this provider.
  • Google Analytics: Used to understand aggregate traffic patterns. Analytics data is not linked to the amounts you enter into the calculator.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Google Analytics may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure site usage. The calculator itself does not store any information - including a language preference cookie - in your browser.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@refundjapan.com.
